Frequently Asked Questions

DERSec LabTest Pro FAQ

Concise answers to common questions about DER certification testing, interoperability, laboratory hardware, and automated test execution.

What is DERSec LabTest Pro?

LabTest Pro is DER certification and interoperability testing software that coordinates laboratory hardware, standards-based procedures, DER communications, measurement capture, and pass/fail assessment in one automated test environment.

What standards can LabTest Pro help test?

LabTest Pro supports certification-oriented workflows for IEEE 1547.1 and UL 1741 SB, including repeatable test execution, measurements, timing evidence, and result assessment.

What laboratory hardware can LabTest Pro coordinate?

LabTest Pro can coordinate categories including AC or grid simulators, programmable DC sources, data acquisition systems, power analyzers, real-time or HIL systems, and equipment-under-test interfaces, depending on available drivers and the LabTest version.

Who uses LabTest Pro?

Typical users include DER manufacturers, Nationally Recognized Testing Laboratories and other certification labs, power electronics engineers, system test engineers, and interoperability or quality-assurance teams.

How does LabTest Pro automate DER certification testing?

The workflow is Configure, Connect, Execute, Measure, and Validate. Test parameters and limits are configured first, laboratory equipment is connected programmatically, standards-based sequences are executed, measurements are captured, and results are assessed for traceable pass/fail evidence.

Does LabTest Pro support DER interoperability testing?

Yes. LabTest Pro can validate DER measurements, telemetry, settings, controls, and command-response behavior alongside physical power-system performance using standards-aware test logic and protocol intelligence.
